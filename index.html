// how it works

Three steps. No cloud in sight.

Contour follows the same flow every time — pick your files, choose how it looks, generate. Nothing is uploaded at any point.

01

Pick your files

A FIT file from your Garmin or GPS device, and the matching video clip straight from your camera.

02

Set your layout and sync

Choose where the overlay sits. Tap the map to mark where your video starts, or let Contour work it out automatically.

03

Generate

Your footage renders right there on your phone, overlay baked in, ready to view, share, or split for Strava.

// what's inside

Built for the moment, not the upload.

Privacy by construction

Your footage never leaves your phone.

There's no server, no account, no upload step. Contour reads your files, renders the overlay, and that's it — the same privacy model as editing a photo in your gallery app.

The hill ahead

A live elevation chart tracks exactly where you are on the climb as the video plays.

Ready for Strava

Split any finished video into 30-second segments, saved where Strava's own uploader can find them.

Speaks your language

Contour's interface switches fully between six languages, with the choice remembered permanently.
